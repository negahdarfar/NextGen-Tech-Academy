# Research and fact-check standard

Every educational video topic must have a research pack before scripting factual claims.

## Minimum evidence record

Each source records:
- Stable ID used by claim citations
- Canonical HTTP(S) URL
- Publisher
- Original publication date
- Date accessed by the researcher

Each factual claim records its text and one or more source IDs. The validator requires at least two source records from distinct domains and rejects claims with missing or unknown evidence references.

## Editorial rules

- Prefer primary sources: research papers, official technical documentation, standards bodies, company engineering posts, and government or university publications.
- Use independent secondary reporting to contextualize claims; do not treat multiple articles repeating one press release as independent confirmation.
- Separate measured results, company claims, expert interpretation, and speculation.
- Preserve uncertainty, limitations, dates, units, and the exact scope of a study.
- Never invent a citation or present an unverified claim as fact.

The current validator checks structure and traceability only. A human/editor must still assess source credibility, independence, factual accuracy, and whether each source actually supports its cited claim.
