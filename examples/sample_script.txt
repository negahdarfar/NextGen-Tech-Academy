Today, we are exploring how modern AI agents work, why they are different from ordinary chatbots, and how they can plan and use tools to complete multi-step tasks. We will begin with the basic architecture, including the model, instructions, memory, and the tools an agent can call. Then we will follow a practical example: an agent receives a goal, breaks it into smaller steps, gathers information, checks its progress, and decides what to do next. We will also look at the limits of autonomy. Agents can make mistakes, misunderstand instructions, or rely on outdated information, so reliable systems need clear permissions, source verification, and human oversight for important decisions. Finally, we will compare different agent designs and explain how to evaluate their usefulness in real products. By the end, you will understand the core components of an AI agent and know what questions to ask before trusting one with a complex task.
